The loft scene

One house that grows a floor at a time. Every bird has a room and every add-on has one fixed place, so the scene looks composed with any mix of birds and purchases.

How it grows

The same six purchases in order. Each one fills its own place and nothing else shifts.
1. Day one
Three rooms under one roof. Two birds and the first egg.
2. Rooftop birdbath · 220 seeds
Lands in the left slot. The house does not move.
3. Perch 4 · 250 seeds
A second floor goes up. Its spare room hangs a sign.
4. Perch 5 · 400 seeds
The spare room opens. The roof stays put.

New add-ons, prices to set. Right slot, and along the wall.
6. Weathervane
New add-on, price to set. On the roof peak.

Day and night

Five looks, picked by how high the sun is where you live. Sunrise and sunset last about half an hour each.
Sunrise
Sun up to 6 degrees high, morning. Birds wake. Lights go off.
Day
Sun above 6 degrees. Lights off. A few windows lit.
Sunset
Sun under 6 degrees, evening. Lights come on. Windows start to light.
Dusk and dawn
Sun just below the horizon. Birds sleep, doorways glow, first stars.
Night
Sun 6 degrees under. Moon, stars, a lit city. Everyone asleep.
What drives it
The real sun at your loft, from the same sun maths the flights use. No clock settings, no time zones to get wrong.
Birds sleep when the sun is down, the same moment a bird in the air has to roost. They wake at sunrise.
Lights are on from sunset to sunrise. Bulbs glow and twinkle. By day they hang unlit.
Only colour changes. Nothing moves between times of day. Each piece is the day drawing, tinted; lit windows, bulbs, doorways, moon and stars are left untinted. Outlines stay ink.
Words on the sky switch to cream at dusk and night.
When the time of day changes with the app open, cross-fade over two seconds.

Rules

One ground line
Everything stands on the deck or on a perch. Nothing overlaps anything it is not standing on.
The house is the backdrop
Birds sit against flat yellow, never against windows, so the city behind can be as lively as we like.
Fixed slots
Each add-on has exactly one place. Buying one never moves another, so every combination is already composed.
Grows by floors
Three rooms to a floor, newest floor on top, top floor centred. A sixth perch would start a third floor; add 80 pt of sky for it.
Scales with the screen
From 320 to 430 pt wide the art scales as one picture. The 128 pt sky band on top stays as it is.
The scene is live
Each room shows what its bird is doing. Tap a room for that bird, the birdbath or cart for the store.

COO ASSET REFERENCE

Map style

One continuous, accurate map in the sticker-book look, from a few blocks to several states. Real geography comes from OpenStreetMap vector tiles; this sheet is the style we paint it with.

One map, four zoom bands

Detail arrives as you zoom in. Colours and outlines never change, so it reads as one map.
Zoom 4 to 6: country
States, big lakes, coast, mountains. Multi-day routes and roost markers.
Zoom 7 to 10: region
Towns, rivers, parks. Flights measured in hours.
Zoom 11 to 14: city
Urban tone, neighbourhood names, main roads, bridges.
Zoom 15 to 17: block
Every building as a block, street names, food.

Night, build, camera, privacy

Night style
Swaps land and water after local sunset. Same layers, same data.
How it is built
One vector tile source in the OpenMapTiles schema, drawn by MapLibre GL JS with our own style file. The style is a list of rules like the table below, so the map is real, current OpenStreetMap geography in this look at every zoom, with no seams between levels.

The four pictures here are hand-drawn targets for that style, not screenshots of it.
Camera
Opening a flight fits both ends of the route with 48px of padding, then eases toward the bird. Pinch and the plus and minus buttons zoom freely from 3 to 17. The bird stays the same size on screen at every zoom; only the map changes scale.

A scale bar sits bottom left: 500 ft, 10 mi, 100 mi.
Location precision
A friend set to City is placed at a roost near the middle of their city, and the map will not zoom past 12 on their end of the route. A friend set to Block is placed at the roost nearest them and unlocks zoom 17. No end point is ever an address.

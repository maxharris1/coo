Construction

Five jointed parts
Tail, legs, torso with wing, head, neck. Each is its own group with its own pivot, so the head can bob, the wing shrug and the tail flick independently. Always facing right; flip the whole drawing to face left.
Outline and eye
3px ink outline at the 120-wide master size, scaled with the bird. Eye is orange, 6.5 radius, with a small pupil set forward so he always looks slightly alarmed.
Three colours each
Body, wing and collar. A new bird is a new set of three. Breeds add one shape: a fan, a balloon, a hood, some curls.

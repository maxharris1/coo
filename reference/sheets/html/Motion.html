COO ASSET REFERENCE

Motion

Birds are animated as jointed characters: parts overlap, anticipate and follow through. Everything here runs live, and the same keyframes ship as motion.css.

Principles

Jointed, not sliding
A bird is five parts with their own pivots. Nothing moves as one rigid sticker except the slow float in flight.
Squash, then stretch
Every jump is announced by a squash and paid off with a stretch. Landings overshoot and settle.
Different clocks
Idle parts run on 1.9, 4.2, 4.6, 5.5 and 6 second loops, so the combined motion takes minutes to repeat.
Takeoff is the only delay
About 1.5 seconds from tap to tracker. With reduced motion on, every loop stops and takeoff is a straight cut.

Full sequences

How the keyframes below combine.
Idle
Head bob, blink, breathing, tail flick, wing shrug and swaying gear, all on different clocks so the loop never looks like a loop.
Flight cycle
Wings flip through the body 50ms apart. The body pitches against the beat, the tail counter-swings, and the whole bird floats on a slower wave.
Takeoff
Small stretch up, deep crouch, spring. At the top of the spring the perched drawing swaps for the flying one, which climbs out with dust and a loose feather behind.
Landing
Glide in, swap to the perched drawing a few pixels up, drop into a squash, rebound, settle.

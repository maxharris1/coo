COO ASSET REFERENCE

Voice and copy

How the app talks, the notification set, and a starter library of flight log lines. All of it ships as JSON in the asset pack.

Five rules

  1. The bird is the hero. Write about what the bird did, not what the system did.
  2. Deadpan beats wacky. State the absurd thing plainly, in short sentences.
  3. Nothing bad happens to a bird. Late, distracted, asleep, sulking. Never hurt, never gone.
  4. Buttons say exactly what happens: Release Gerald, Send a search party, Text it to Maya.
  5. Numbers are real. Miles, minutes and odds come from the flight, never from a joke.

Empty and error states

No flights
No birds in the air. Suspiciously quiet.
Empty sill
Nobody on your sill yet. Send the first note.
No rested birds
Everyone is asleep or eating. Feed someone, or wait for a nap to end.
Offline
You are offline. Birds keep flying. The map catches up when you are back.
Expired link
That link has expired. The bird went home with the note.
Place not found
Could not find that place. Try a neighborhood or a city.

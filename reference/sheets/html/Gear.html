COO ASSET REFERENCE

Gear

All fourteen closet items: on the bird, as a closet icon, with slot, price and effect. Overlays are drawn in the bird's own coordinates so they fit every bird and every upright pose.

Rules

Shared by the flock
One closet. Any bird can wear any item, one bird at a time.
Four slots
Head, neck, leg, and back for later. One item per slot.
One job each
Every item changes one number or one behaviour, in plain words.
Never punishing
Wrong gear slows a bird. It does not hurt it or lose the note.
